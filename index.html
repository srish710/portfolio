REU: MD Anderson

June 2025
BrandingWeb

At MD Anderson Cancer Center, I completed over 400 hours of in vitro cancer research investigating how human malignant peripheral nerve sheath tumor (MPNST) cell lines respond to liposomal formulations of olaparib, a PARP inhibitor. I cultured and treated multiple MPNST cell lines, then used PCR, RT-qPCR, and fluorescence microscopy to evaluate treatment response and examine how different tumor-associated mutations influenced cellular behavior and drug sensitivity. By comparing results across genetically distinct cell lines, I explored whether specific mutations were associated with differences in therapeutic response, resistance, and treatment-related cellular changes. I performed experiments under BSL-2 protocols, documented and analyzed findings, troubleshot experimental challenges, and contributed the results to a research poster presentation. This experience strengthened my understanding of targeted cancer therapy, drug-delivery systems, molecular testing, and the importance of a tumor’s genetic profile in determining treatment effectiveness.
