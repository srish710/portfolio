Selected Projects

NASAOrbit

As part of the Build4Good hackathon, I developed, alongside a team, a website to moniter and track vital signs of astronauts as according to NASA battery tests, described in HERA studies. The website has a user and admin end, with admin end offering downloadable files containing all team member statistics.

DesignHTMLCSS

DreamBreathe

As a part of the Product@TAMU hackathon, I developed, alongside a team, a product concept for a smartCPAP machine called DreamBreathe. This machine concept comes with a fully developed mock-up and design, as well as the framework for the full associated app, which offers sleep statistic readings, as well as notification settings in cases of emergencies.
